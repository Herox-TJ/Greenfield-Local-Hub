Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Important: By using Greenfield Local Hub, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. We are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Billing and delivery address
  • Payment information (processed securely via third parties)
  • Account credentials (email and password hash)
  • Profile preferences and account settings

Transaction Information

  • Order history and purchase details
  • Payment and refund information processed through third-party providers
  • Loyalty points and rewards history

Usage Information

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring URLs and search terms
  • Approximate geographic location based on IP

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders and payments
  • Create and manage your account
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Communicate with you about products, services, and promotions
  • Improve our platform and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

With Local Producers

We share your name and delivery details with producers to fulfil your orders. This information is used only for order fulfilment and is not stored beyond the necessary period.

With Service Providers

We work with trusted third parties who assist in operating our website, processing payments, and delivering services. These providers are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Greenfield Local Hub, our customers, or others.

4. Your Privacy Rights

Under UK and EU data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request corrections or updates to inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your data for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@greenfieldlocalhub.co.uk.

5. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures:

  • All passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
  • Database access is restricted and monitored
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure data transmission using HTTPS encryption
Please Note: While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our platform. These include session cookies (essential for site functionality), preference cookies (remembering your settings), and analytics cookies (understanding how the site is used).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our site.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@greenfieldlocalhub.co.uk
  • Post: 123 Green Street, Greenfield, GF1 2AB, United Kingdom
